## 安装方法

将两个压缩包中的文件解压后，直接复制到你的 DCS 保存游戏目录：

`保存的游戏\DCS`

如果你使用 `-w` 参数启动 DCS，实际目录名称可能不同。

如果是 DCS 专用服务器，目录通常为：

`保存的游戏\DCS.server`

请确保两个压缩包中的文件都正确合并到对应的 DCS 保存游戏目录中。

如果你不确定如何操作，可以把这些安装说明发给 AI，让它根据你的 DCS 安装方式一步一步指导你。

## Installation

Extract the contents of both archives and copy them directly into your DCS Saved Games directory:

`Saved Games\DCS`

If you launch DCS with the `-w` parameter, the actual folder name may be different.

For a DCS Dedicated Server, the directory is usually:

`Saved Games\DCS.server`

Make sure the contents of both archives are merged into the appropriate DCS Saved Games directory.

If you're not sure how to install it, you can paste these instructions into an AI assistant and ask it to guide you through the process step by step based on your DCS setup.
